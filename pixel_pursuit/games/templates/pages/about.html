Pixel Pursuit

Pixel Pursuit is a web-hosted, arcade-style gaming platform that features a collection of independent mini-games. Players aim to complete all available mini-games to unlock achievements and earn recognition. The platform supports repeated gameplay, allowing users to improve scores and attempt full completion multiple times. Players who successfully complete all mini-games will have their usernames publicly recognized, encouraging competition, replayability, and long-term engagement.
